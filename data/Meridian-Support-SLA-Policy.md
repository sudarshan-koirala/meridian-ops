# Meridian Labs - Customer Support SLA Policy (v3.2)

Owner: Head of Customer Success
Effective: 1 July 2026
Applies to: All customer tiers (Free, Pro, Enterprise) in all regions (NA, Europe, APAC, LATAM)

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the response and resolution targets for customer support tickets at Meridian Labs.
Every ticket must get a first human response inside the target time for its tier.

## 2. Response targets by tier

| Tier       | First response target | Resolution target | Coverage                      |
|------------|-----------------------|-------------------|-------------------------------|
| Free       | 24 hours              | 72 hours          | Business hours, Europe team   |
| Pro        | 8 hours               | 48 hours          | Business hours, regional team |
| Enterprise | 4 hours               | 24 hours          | 24/7, regional team           |

## 3. Regional coverage
- NA tickets go to the NA team (Austin office).
- Europe tickets go to the Europe team (Helsinki office).
- LATAM tickets go to the LATAM team (Bogota office).
- APAC tickets are routed to the Europe queue. Meridian Labs has no APAC support team yet.
  The Europe team picks up APAC tickets from 08:00 CET. A local APAC team is planned for Q2 2027.

## 4. Escalation
- If a Pro or Enterprise ticket has no first response after 75% of its target time, the system escalates it to the regional team lead.
- If a ticket breaches its first response target, the Customer Success Manager must contact the account inside 24 hours.
- Two or more breaches for one account in 30 days put the account on the churn-risk watch list.

## 5. Measurement
- First response time = hours from ticket creation to the first human reply.
- SLA compliance is reported every month per region and per tier.
- Target: 95% of tickets meet the first response target in every region.

## 6. Review
This policy is reviewed every 6 months. Next review: January 2027.
